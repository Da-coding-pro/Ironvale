The game

Built for quick, loud fights

The Titan Circuit is an industrial ring world at war. The Ironvale Compact fights the corrupted Hollow Choir for its foundries, and every battle is fought by mechs.

⚙

40 unique mechs

Seven rarities, from Common to Ultra Legendary and Elemental. No two share an attack or a dash.

⚔

Every way to fight

1v1 Duel, 2v2, 3v3, Showdown, Titan Fusion, Ranked from Bronze to Unmatched, and PvE contracts.

⇄

True cross-play

Windows, Android and browser players meet in the same matches, with parties, friends and cloud saves.

★

Real progression

Trophy road, power levels, Forged Rewards, daily quests, a season pass, skins and signature gears.

◎

Hidden pilots

Godly pilots fight on foot and call in their own mega mech when the fight turns.

✋

Play your way

Keyboard and mouse, a controller, or twin-stick touch controls on your phone.
